# Dashboard surface brief

Scope: `dashboard/` single page, now a scroll-told 3D site that is also the live dashboard. Visitor mode: **Persuade** (owner: "an awwwards-winning website", igloo-level finish, Three.js, scroll storytelling where the object dismantles). Audience: Reactive Omni Buildathon judges; their action is to verify: open the loop's transactions on explorers. Proof: live chain events + bench evidence JSON; every number links to a tx or a source. Constraints: static (GitHub Pages), public RPCs from the browser, LIVE vs LOCAL labelled, reduced-motion and no-WebGL paths, readable in a 1440×810 screen recording and on a 390 px phone.

Direction: owner-pinned (option A of three presented in chat: "The Instrument", igloo × chess clock). No concept-seed roll: a user-pinned direction beats the roll. Build path: code-led (WebGL scene; the technique is built, not pictured).

## Direction contract

THESIS: One object, the settlement loop as a digital tournament chess clock: two LCDs, Across's window against Snapsettle's measured repay. Scrolling takes it apart: the circuit inside is the protocol, each chip a deployed contract, each trace a leg with its measured time. Refuses the category default: dark neon dashboard of KPI tiles and charts.

OWN-WORLD: Cold fog over snow (sky #93a0ae → horizon #d3d9df, snow #e9edf1, visible drifts and ridges). The clock is a real tournament object: soft-touch graphite body (#16191c), brushed-aluminium faceplate with four screws, glossy recessed bezels, backlit LCDs (DSEG7 segments on #c4d0bd), aluminium rocker with rubber caps, an orange status LED. Inside: a solder-mask green board with gold pads. One signal colour, flag orange (#ff5a1f), only for live things: a moving pulse, the LED, a fresh number. Studio lighting (softbox reflections on a black environment), ambient occlusion, filmic tone. Type: Funnel Display light for headlines, Martian Mono for annotations and figures, DSEG7 on the LCDs. Annotations sit on hairlines pinned to parts with crosshairs; values scramble in.

STORY: The judge sees two clocks: 2 hours against 1 second. Scrolling opens the clock: four chips named as the four contracts with their chains and addresses; the traces light leg by leg with p50/p95; a tier switch shows the three finality policies; refusals show orders the circuit rejected; the scoresheet lists every order with its four transactions. The judge leaves with: it runs, live, and here are the hashes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed fog scene; the clock at three-quarter view, centre-right, about 55% of the viewport width. Left LCD counts down from 2:00:00 (labelled Across challenge window, source linked); right LCD holds the measured p50 fill → repaid. Top-left: wordmark and one-line claim. Top-right: three chain heights ticking live. Bottom-left: the headline in two lines. Bottom-right: the primary action "Verify a full loop" (opens the four explorer links of the latest settled order) and a scroll cue.

FORM: Exploded-view product film (igloo-grade scene + annotation UI), pinned by the owner. seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: "Open your own order, live": a judge's wallet opens a 0.25 USDC order and the clock follows it through its four real transactions (LCD counts, traces light leg by leg, the LED flashes on repay), labelled LIVE ON TESTNET. "Run one live order" does the same through the demo service; "Replay a recorded loop" is labelled REPLAY everywhere. Scroll scrubs the dismantle and reassembly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
