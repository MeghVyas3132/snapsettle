    Open it up.

    Inside are four contracts on three chains. The user's money waits in an escrow on Base. The solver fills the order on Unichain from its own pocket. A Reactive contract on Lasna sees both events, checks the fill against the order, and calls back to Base to pay the solver.

    Nobody runs a relayer for this. The callback is a Reactive transaction.

    Every leg, timed.

    Median and 95th percentile over the live loops of orders that settle on sight. Each time comes from the clock that is hardest to fool: the chain's own blocks where possible, the solver's own receipt for the fill.

    Finality is a setting.

    Reactive acts on what it sees, even if a chain later reorganises. So each order carries its own policy: small ones settle on sight, larger ones wait. Solvers read the wait from our ERC-7683 resolver as estimatedDelaySeconds.

    It also says no.

    A fill with the wrong terms is not paid. A late fill reverts. An order nobody fills goes back to its owner.
